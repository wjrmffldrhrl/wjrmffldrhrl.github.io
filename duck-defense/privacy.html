Duck Defense Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

The current iOS and Android version of Duck Defense by 10duckgames is an offline single-player game. The developer does not collect personal information or gameplay data through the app or send it to a developer server.

Information processed by the app

The app has no account registration, ads, in-app purchases, analytics SDK, or online service. Coins, units, waves, and other game state are kept in device memory while playing and reset when the app restarts. The app does not access contacts, location, camera, or microphone data.

Stores and this website

Apple and Google handle information associated with their app stores under their own privacy policies. This page is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical information when you visit it under GitHub's policy.
